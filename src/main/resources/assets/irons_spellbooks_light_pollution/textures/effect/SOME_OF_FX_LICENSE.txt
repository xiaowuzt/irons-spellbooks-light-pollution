Some of FX assets
=================

`noise_tile.png` and `singularity_core.png` are from Some of FX
(https://github.com/YangMao-Minister/some_of_fx), MIT licensed,
Copyright (c) 2026 Pizuka.

`noise_tile.png` is used unchanged. It backs the lightning and shockwave
distortion: the procedural hash noise this mod used first has visible banding at
low frequencies, and the bolts read as smooth ribbons because of it.

`singularity_core.png` is `textures/item/singularity_bomb.png` renamed. The
nested-cube geometry it is mapped onto is a hand-rewrite of that project's
`models/item/sigularity_bomb.json` for Forge 1.20.1: the original targets
`format_version` 1.21.6 and the newer `items/` definition format, neither of
which loads here, so only the UV layout and the cube nesting were carried over.
